# Nandverse economy boundaries

Current stage: **2 — launch**, following the confirmed 2026-10-07 OATH creation. [Actual parameters and receipt](OATH-RELEASE.md). Consumption, staking, additional games and revenue-funded buybacks remain planned.

Neon Reliquary is currently the only Nandverse game. Free gameplay, shared NAND/LATCH materials and OATH are separate flows. Future games can use the same material contracts and wallet inventory, but multi-game integration is planned rather than shipped.

Material quotes distinguish required units, actual wallet inventory (unknown without a connected wallet), shortages, material sale proceeds, per-type mint protocol fees, tapeout fees, gas and the quote block. No purchase occurs on quotation or cancellation. Existing mainnet material and processor contracts remain unchanged.

## Five cumulative OATH stages

1. Before launch: free gameplay and optional material minting/manufacturing exist. OATH preparation creates no supply, trading, consumption or staking claim.
2. Curve launch: real issuance starts bonding-curve trading and configured trading-tax allocation. Curve quote funds belong to the launch mechanism; they are not game or project operating revenue. Initial purchase is zero.
3. Graduated, without consumption or staking: curve funds seed DEX liquidity; eligible DEX trading can continue the tax flow. Operating revenue buybacks and batch burning start only when implemented, funded and verified.
4. Consumption, without staking: only implemented self-operated paid services accept OATH. Returned consumption coins and net buyback coins join one pending-burn pool before batch burning.
5. Consumption and staking: actual staking benefits and principal return must be implemented independently. Staking principal and refundable obligations stay outside revenue and burnable funds.

Author orders use the currency specified by the author, for example OKB. They do not require OATH conversion or project advances. Project operating revenue is accounted for separately; the revenue categories and adjustable buyback percentage will be set before the first real buyback. This percentage is independent of the fixed launch tax split.

Recovered coins are pooled and burned in batches, not redistributed as rewards. Report recovered, pending-burn and actually burned amounts separately. Do not equate a transfer to a dead address with a verified reduction in total supply. No buyback, consumption or staking is currently open.
